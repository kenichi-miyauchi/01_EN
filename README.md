# 01 An Integrated Approach to Addressing Multiple SDG Challenges

## Securing Safe and Sanitary Water and Improving Livelihoods through Agroforestry, Reducing Child Labor, and Promoting CO₂ Sequestration

As the 2030 deadline for achieving the SDGs approaches, many challenges still remain.

Water, nutrition, poverty, child labor, and loss of educational opportunities are not independent issues; they are interconnected.

In addition, climate change risks such as heat waves, droughts, and floods are increasing as global warming progresses due to rising CO₂ concentrations.

It is also important not to overlook the fact that climate change caused by CO₂ emissions from developed countries is affecting children and communities in developing countries.

For approximately 20 years, I have supported UN-related organizations and NGOs working to support children.

Among various forms of support, I have felt that the child sponsorship programs provided by child-support NGOs offer relatively comprehensive support.

These programs aim to improve people's livelihoods through community development while enabling children to attend school without engaging in child labor.

They seek to break the vicious cycle in which poverty leads to child labor, loss of educational opportunities, and poverty in the next generation.

Rather than addressing each issue individually, I considered whether there might be a way to approach multiple challenges simultaneously.

Renewable energy  
→ Seawater desalination / Water purification  
→ Access to safe and sanitary water / Reduced burden of water collection  
→ Water-saving technologies  
→ Agroforestry  
→ Improved livelihoods (community development)  
→ Reduction of child labor  
→ Recovery of educational opportunities  
→ Forest development  
→ CO₂ sequestration

This is an **“Integrated Approach to Addressing Multiple SDG Challenges”** that combines existing technologies and approaches.

Relevant SDGs  
01 No Poverty  
02 Zero Hunger  
03 Good Health and Well-being  
04 Quality Education  
05 Gender Equality  
06 Clean Water and Sanitation  
07 Affordable and Clean Energy  
08 Decent Work and Economic Growth  
13 Climate Action  
15 Life on Land

I am not an expert in these fields, and this approach cannot be realized by one person alone, particularly in terms of funding, research, and practical demonstration.

If any part of this idea can be utilized or may lead to further research or demonstration, I hope that experts will make use of it.
